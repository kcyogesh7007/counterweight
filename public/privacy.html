Privacy Policy

Counterweight: Balance Puzzle · last updated 25 November 2026

Counterweight is a free puzzle game, on the web and as an Android app. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, stars, streaks, a half-finished daily puzzle, and the sound setting) is saved only on your own device, in the game's local storage. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data deletes it.

Anonymous visit counting

To learn how many people play, the game uses GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-friendly counter. It uses no cookies and no advertising IDs, and it can't identify you. When you open the game, and when you finish a puzzle, use a hint or share a result, it receives:

  • which screen or event it was (for example "daily puzzle solved"),
  • the website that sent you here, if any,
  • your browser or device type and screen size,
  • your country, worked out from your internet address. The address itself is not stored.

These counts are only used to see how the game is doing and to improve it. They are never sold or shared, and they are not linked to you. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Permissions

The Android app only uses the internet permission, for the visit counting above. The game itself works fully offline.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/kcyogesh7007/counterweight/issues.
